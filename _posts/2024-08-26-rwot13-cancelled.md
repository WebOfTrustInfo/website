It is with great regret that we announce the cancellation of Rebooting the Web of Trust 13 Ventura (RWOT13), which was scheduled to take place from October 7-11, 2024, in Ventura, California.

 <!--more-->

Despite our best efforts and the excitement surrounding decentralized identity technologies, we have faced significant challenges in securing the necessary support to host this event at the standard our community expects and deserves. After careful consideration, we have made the difficult decision to cancel RWOT13.

We understand this news is disappointing, and we deeply apologize for any inconvenience this may cause. Refunds for tickets purchased will be processed automatically. If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us at treasurer@weboftrust.info.

We appreciate your interest in decentralized identity and your support for Rebooting the Web of Trust. We look forward to continuing this important work together in the future and will keep you informed about upcoming events and initiatives.

Thank you for your understanding and ongoing commitment.
