We are now accepting submissions for advance reading papers for the 2024 Rebooting the Web of Trust 13 design workshop in Ventura, California, from October 7-11.

<!--more-->

Digital identity is in a state of disruption. Between the exponential acceleration of artificial intelligence and the new EU Digital Identity Wallet becoming law, the pressure and opportunities for breakthroughs in decentralized identity is unprecedented.

RWOT is the premier venue for deep discussions with like-minded individuals focused on shaping the future of decentralized identity, digital humanity, and civil rights. 

* What’s happening today in decentralized identity? 
* What do we envision for the future? 
* How do we bridge the gap? 

These are the topics that participants bring to the table to merge and mature into industry-changing white papers and repositories. Submitting an advance reading is not only a great way to get a discount, it’s a great way to contribute your voice into the global conversation about how decentralized technologies will restructure modern identity.

Advance readings of one to two pages can be submitted on our [RWOT13 Ventura GitHub](https://github.com/WebOfTrustInfo/rwot13-ventura/tree/main/advance-readings).

Submitting an advance reading is a excellent way to seed topics and shape the focus of our design workshop. We offer significant discounts to all participants who submit an advance reading, ideally just one-to-two pages describing a topic that might be of interest to others. An advance-reading paper could focus on your area of expertise, highlighting the information that you can offer to other workshop participants, or it could explore on a topic you’re interested in learning about. Whatever topic you choose, an advance reading is your opportunity to kickstart the conversation and introduce your ideas to others.

For more details, view our [advance reading primer](https://github.com/WebOfTrustInfo/rwot13-ventura/blob/main/advance-readings/advance-reading-primer.md).

## Deadlines

Early Bird deadline for ticket purchases is August 9th.
- To qualify for a discount, advanced readings are due one week earlier on August 2nd.
 
Regular Purchase deadline for ticket purchases is September 13th.
- To qualify for a discount, advanced readings are due one week earlier on September 6th.
 
Be a pioneer in shaping the future of decentralized identity. Submit your advance reading paper today and secure your place at RWOT 13 in Ventura! Don't miss this opportunity to drive the conversation and make a lasting impact.

More information on tickets and travel at [RWOT13 on Eventbrite](https://rwot13.eventbrite.com).

## Scholarships and Stipends Available

We offer travel stipends and ticket scholarships for select attendees bringing diverse perspectives to the event. To apply for either stipend or scholarship, you must submit an advance reading paper by August 2nd so we can make our selections before the August 9th Early Bird Deadline.

To learn more, email <a href="mailto:treasurer@weboftrust.info">treasurer@weboftrust.info</a>.
